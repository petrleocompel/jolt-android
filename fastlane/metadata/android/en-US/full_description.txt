Jolt is an independent, open-source companion app for Pavlok wearables (Pavlok 2 and Pavlok 3; Shock Clock Max connects but cannot fire yet). Not affiliated with or endorsed by Pavlok Inc.

Pair your device over Bluetooth and take control:
• Fire zap, vibration or beep at any intensity and repeat count, with tap, hold or confirm firing per stimulus
• Read live device status: battery, firmware and connection
• Configure what the physical buttons do, and poke a friend from one of them
• Set alarms that live on the device itself, so they fire even when your phone is off, or phone alarms that ring over the lock screen
• Add a wake-up guarantee: solve a puzzle, do jumping jacks or scan a QR code before an alarm stops

Trade jolts with friends:
• Add friends by handle, invite code or QR, and set exactly what each of them may send you
• Cap intensity and set a cooldown per stimulus, and decide whether their scripts may poke you too
• Pokes arrive as a notification and fire on your wrist wherever you are
• A Do Not Disturb switch mutes every incoming poke

Controlling your own device needs no account and works fully offline. Friends and pokes run on Jolt Server, which is open source and can be self-hosted; push notifications are end-to-end encrypted on their way through the relay.

Optional: sign in with a Pavlok account to poke your existing Pavlok friends. That uses Pavlok's own cloud service.
